# Phase 2: twenty prompts written the way Orainge's creators (16–24, making scenes and games)
# type them: short, casual, often with a style word. Thirteen are everyday asks; seven are
# known weak spots (glass, liquids, thin parts, whole scenes) that users will ask for anyway,
# so the go/no-go counts them too. None repeat the starter set.
#
#   modal run --detach workers/modal_app.py::make_set --prompts workers/test-sets/phase2.txt --name phase2 --pictures-only
#   modal run --detach workers/modal_app.py::make_set --prompts workers/test-sets/phase2.txt --name phase2 --picks "1=2,5=3"
#
# The first round (30 Sep 2026) used the picks "1=4,2=3,3=2,4=4,5=4,6=3,7=2,8=4,9=2,10=3,11=2,12=4,
# 13=2,14=4,15=4,16=2,17=3,18=4,19=2,20=1"; phase2.verdicts.json holds the reviewers' verdicts
# (make_gallery.py --verdicts). 10 of 20 were publishable as they stood, against a bar of 16.
#
# Phase 4 (30 Sep 2026) made the same pictures again with its fixes (ops/rerun_set.py on the ai-ops
# branch): with the original seeds 11 of 20 were publishable, with the next seeds 10 of 20, and 12
# of 20 with either. Verdicts: phase2.phase4-verdicts.json (make_gallery.py --per-group).
#
# Phase 5 (1 Oct 2026) did the same with smoothed shading normals, the out-of-memory fallback and the
# picture painted onto the side it shows: 12 of 20 publishable with Phase 4's seeds, 11 of 20 with the
# next seeds, 13 of 20 with either. Verdicts: phase2.phase5-verdicts.json.
#
# Phase 6 (1-2 Oct 2026) made the finals with the Pixal3D recipe instead (workers/README.md, "The
# recipe"), in a staging app: 11 of 20 publishable with Phase 4's seeds, 10 of 20 with the next seeds,
# 13 of 20 with either (everyday 11 of 13, weak spots 2 of 7; Phase 5: 8 and 5). Side by side and
# blinded, four more reviewers judging both finals of each pair preferred Phase 5's in 24 of 40 pairs
# (even on everyday asks, 13 to 13; Phase 5 on weak spots, 11 to 3), so finals stay TRELLIS.2's.
# Verdicts: phase2.phase6-verdicts.json; the pairs: phase2.phase6-paired.json.
#
# Phase 7 (2 Oct 2026) worked on textures, on TRELLIS.2's own shapes, in staging apps. Ways to make the
# one texture better (CFG 3, 24 steps, MV-Adapter's image+geometry views baked in or steering the
# texture flow) didn't beat production's in blinded pairs. But the texture is a lottery: drawn three
# more times on the same shape, four reviewers per texture found the best of the four publishable for
# 16 of 20 in each of three runs (Phase 4's seeds, the next seeds, Phase 4's with other noise), against
# 10, 11 and 14 for the final's own texture (15.8, 15.7 and 15.8 when two reviewers pick and two others
# judge). Image statistics and a self-hosted VLM judge (Qwen3-VL-8B: 27.5 of 40 against 25) couldn't
# find the best one reliably, so the Studio offers the creator the three more textures to pick from
# (texture options). Verdicts and picks: phase2.phase7-textures.json.
#
